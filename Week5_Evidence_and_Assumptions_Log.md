## 5. Initial enquiry taxonomy

### University-wide categories

1. Admission and enrolment.
2. Tuition and payment.
3. Credit registration.
4. Graduation registration and clearance.
5. Scholarship eligibility and application.
6. Timetable, class changes and full-class requests.
7. EMS or other university-system issues.
8. Correct department and contact identification.

### CSE pilot categories

1. Missing credits.
2. Changing classes or rearranging schedules.
3. Requesting admission to a full class.
4. Project-topic registration.
5. Selecting or revising a project topic.
6. Selecting or contacting a supervisor.
7. Project-registration revisions.
8. Project-period deadlines and required documents.

These categories are an initial team taxonomy, not measured frequency rankings. Frequency should be established from pilot intake data or coded historical enquiries.

## Presentation rule

Every Week 5 slide should distinguish among:

- **Recorded evidence:** supported by the Customer Experience research material.
- **Team estimate:** supplied or revalidated by the group in October 2026.
- **Proposed target:** a threshold for the MVP, not an achieved result.
- **Unknown:** explicitly converted into a discovery or measurement backlog item.
