# VNUK: Current State, Problem Domains and Capability Assessment

**Scope:** BMS and CSE. Research conducted approximately December 2025–January 2026. The team confirms that the issues continue, but the numerical results have not been remeasured.

## 1. Current State

| Department | Main findings | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **CSE** | Academic support exists, but guidance and responsiveness vary. Students report confusing course registration, uncertain confirmation and fragmented information about academic milestones. | Supplied interviews include both positive supervision experiences and difficulties. One student recalls a 4–5-day advising response; one alumnus reports a missed graduation-registration notice. These are individual cases, not average performance. |
| **BMS** | Students report laboratory discomfort, insufficient practical time and heavy academic workload. | Among **8 respondents**, **7** report heat-related fatigue or reduced productivity, **4** report insufficient practice time and **6** report heavy workload. Mean lab satisfaction is **3.63/5**, calculated from the original chart. |

**Diagnosis:** both departments face difficulties in accessing support or learning resources. CSE evidence points to inconsistent guidance and administrative coordination. BMS evidence points to laboratory conditions and practical access. The causes should be assessed separately.

**Evidence limits:** CSE’s reported full sample is 15 students and 5 faculty members; the supplied interview files cover 10 students/alumni and one faculty member. BMS results describe a small sample. The lab satisfaction chart corrects the report’s inaccurate “2/5” statement.

## 2. Domain Identification and Shortlisting

### 2.1 Map the domain landscape

| Domain | End-to-end outcome | Evidence coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **Course planning and registration** | Students obtain a valid, confirmed course selection | CSE |
| **Academic advising and enquiry resolution** | Students receive an answer or complete an appropriate referral | Mainly CSE; BMS needs further validation |
| **Thesis registration and supervision** | Students secure a supervisor and complete agreed project milestones | CSE |
| **Graduation eligibility and clearance** | Students confirm eligibility and resolve outstanding requirements | CSE |
| **Practical-learning resource access** | Students obtain equipment, space and supervision for a practical session | BMS and CSE |
| **Facility and equipment maintenance** | Staff resolve a reported fault or condition and verify availability | BMS and CSE complaints; maintenance records unavailable |
| **Learning support** | Students receive support for an identified learning difficulty and reassess understanding | BMS and CSE |
| **Career and research opportunity access** | Students obtain relevant guidance or complete an opportunity application/referral | Mainly CSE |

### 2.2 Curate three candidates for capability assessment

The shortlist uses **problem evidence, a clear student outcome and related cross-functional activities**. Related domains are grouped where they share a coherent journey. This is a selection for deeper assessment, not a final value–feasibility ranking.

| Shortlisted candidate | Domains brought together | Why include it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Student Support & Academic Navigation** | Academic advising and enquiry resolution; the information and referral aspects of learning/career support | CSE interviews show fragmented guidance and uneven responsiveness. A defined enquiry-to-resolution journey allows assessment of ownership and service capacity. BMS needs require further validation. |
| **Practical Learning & Resource Operations** | Practical-resource access and the maintenance coordination needed to deliver sessions | BMS survey data identify practical-time and heat concerns; CSE accounts identify computing constraints. This candidate examines the whole resource-delivery process, beyond booking alone. |
| **Academic Progression & Milestone Management** | Course planning/registration, thesis milestones and graduation clearance | CSE evidence shows friction at several connected academic checkpoints. This candidate examines coordination across the progression journey, beyond a registration interface. |

**Boundaries:** Student Support answers and routes enquiries; Academic Progression owns formal milestone checks and exceptions. Practical Learning covers operational access and maintenance coordination; major capital upgrades require a separate decision. Curriculum redesign, clinical counselling and creation of new employer partnerships remain outside these candidates.

The same incident may inform multiple candidates, but its benefit must not be counted twice. **Choosing one priority domain belongs to Part 4**, after capability assessment. An AI hub, booking app or registration tool would be a solution within a domain.

## 3. Capability Assessment

Ratings reflect available evidence: **Partial** = some capability evidenced; **Gap** = reported shortfall; **Unknown** = insufficient evidence. They are not numerical maturity scores.

| Workshop dimension | Student Support | Practical Learning | Academic Progression |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Roadmap** | Unknown: shared owner and targets | Unknown: approved priorities and funding | Unknown: cross-unit ownership |
| **Talent** | Partial: expertise exists; response capacity varies | Partial: teaching expertise exists; technical coverage unverified | Partial: academic roles exist; delivery time unverified |
| **Operating model** | Gap: inconsistent guidance and routing | Gap: borrowing friction and guidance gaps reported | Gap: fragmented information and manual follow-up |
| **Technology** | Partial: communication channels exist; tracking unclear | Unknown: booking and inventory tools not verified | Partial: portal exists; usability/reliability issues reported |
| **Data** | Gap: information is dispersed | Unknown: capacity, inventory and usage data | Partial: records exist; accuracy and integration unverified |
| **Adoption** | Partial: students access support differently | Unknown: use of a shared workflow | Partial: students use systems alongside workarounds |

**Conclusion:** existing expertise and digital tools provide a foundation, but do not establish readiness for transformation. Before prioritisation, each candidate needs a confirmed owner, available staff time, reliable data and appropriate system access.

**Measures to establish:** enquiry resolution time and repeat contacts; fulfilled lab requests and completed practical hours; registration corrections and unresolved milestone exceptions. No verified operational baselines are currently available.
